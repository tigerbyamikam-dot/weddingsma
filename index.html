About Amikam

Rooted in Tradition, Open to All

I'm Amikam Yalovetzky, a wedding officiant based in Massachusetts with a passion for crafting ceremonies as unique as the people in them. Drawing on the richness of Jewish tradition — its poetry, ritual, and joy — I create ceremonies that honor your story, your values, and your love.

Whether you're a same-sex couple, come from different faith backgrounds, or are simply looking for a ceremony that feels authentically you, I'll work with you to weave together something meaningful, joyful, and unforgettable.

What I Offer

Ceremonies Crafted for You

Every package includes a personal consultation, a fully customized ceremony script, and my presence on your day — fully prepared and wholeheartedly present.

💍

Jewish Ceremony

Incorporates traditional elements — the chuppah, the seven blessings (Sheva Brachot), the ketubah, breaking of the glass — adapted to honor same-sex couples and modern sensibilities.

All couples welcome
🕊

Interfaith Ceremony

A thoughtful blend of two (or more) faith traditions woven into one cohesive ceremony that honors both families and creates something beautifully new.

Any combination of traditions
🌿

Secular / Humanist

A ceremony grounded in your shared values and story — no religious language required. Infused with meaning, poetry, and love without the theology.

Non-religious & spiritual

Elopement & Micro-Wedding
